Real edge treatments on booleans, box and capped_cylinder: blend="round" or "chamfer" with blend_r in mm. A boolean's round is the one a ball of radius blend_r rolls (a true arc at any angle, a true distance near it, nothing added where children share a face, and never outside the children's bounding box); a chamfer is the flat between that ball's contact lines. blend_r must be > 0 when blend is not "sharp", and an explicit blend="sharp" keeps a boolean sharp in a smooth_csg part.

New boundary_blend primitive: a cutting skin through 3-4 boundary curves and guide curves with tangent/normal conditions; its curve and guide control points can be design parameters (differentiable). It is an approximate signed field (exact near the skin; its distance-rate bound is reported as unknown). `make_sdf_closure(..., b_implicit_foot=True)` also makes the derivative of its normal and its second derivatives exact, at a higher compile and run cost.
